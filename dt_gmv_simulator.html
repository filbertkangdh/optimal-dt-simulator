Delivery-time → Gross Profit ROI and Optimal DT Simulator

This is a DT → Gross Profit ROI and Optimal DT Simulator: explore the impact on Gross Profit and CLTV if we improve or worsen delivery time (DT). Pick a country, a base (actual) DT, day type, target OL > 30 minutes to be further reduced scenario to see how logistics operating metrics move, then set a target DT to see the Gross Profit and CLTV impact vs. base's baseline. Please also pick to optimize for Gross Profit ROI or Optimal DT.
Sessions and AOV are Q2’2026 inputs; the underlying model is trained on H2’2025–H1’2026 data.

Optimal DT (Gross Profit + CLTV) = maximize (ΔGross Profit + ΔTotal CLTV), a dollar sum that folds in the reorder-retention channel's lifetime value using Gross Profit.

Optimal DT (GMV + CLTV) = the same, but using GMV instead of Gross Profit as the recurring cash-flow figure.

Overall Gross Profit and CLTV Impacts

P&L from the base DT scenario (base→target DT) and OL scenario (base→target OL) driving every section on this page.
Total GMV = GMV from CVR and Fail Recovery (Reduced DT) + GMV from Fail Recovery (Order Late) — Total Cost = Defect cost + Rider Cost.
Revenue = Total GMV × take rate% (this country's own actual commission/delivery-fee rate — see Appendix).
Gross Profit = Revenue − Total Cost.
Four optimization functions:
GMV ROI = ΔGMV/ΔTotal Cost.
Gross Profit ROI = ΔGross Profit/ΔTotal Cost.
Optimal DT (Gross Profit + CLTV) = Gross Profit + Total CLTV.
Optimal DT (GMV + CLTV) = GMV + Total CLTV.
