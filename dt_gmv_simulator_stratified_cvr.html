Simulator Logic ↗
Model variant: the CVR formula here comes from the 2026-08-25 stratified (zone×daytype, frozen-weight-aggregated) quadratic re-derivation instead of production's single country-level regression — PDT/mean delay/surge stay LIVE (DT-predicted, same as production), healthy availability stays fixed at its historical mean (same as production), winter/ramadan stay as day-type flags (same as production); everything else (GMV/CLTV/optimizer/UTR logic) is byte-identical to dt_gmv_simulator.html. Descriptive re-derivation, not causal — see MEMORY.md.
This is a DT → GMV (Gross Profit) ROI and Optimal DT Simulator: explore the impacts on GMV (Gross Profit) ROI and CLTV if we improve or worsen delivery time (DT).
1. Pick a country, a base (actual) DT, day type, target OL > 30 minutes to be further reduced scenario and target EPH adjustment to see how logistics metrics move,
2. Set a target DT to see the GMV (Gross Profit) and CLTV impact vs. base DT's baseline,
3. Pick the optimization function: (a) GMV ROI, (b) Gross Profit ROI, (c) Optimal DT (GP+CLTV) or (d) Optimal DT (GMV+CLTV) ROI.
Note: Sessions and Average Order Value (AOV) are Q2’2026 inputs and the underlying model is trained on H2’2025–H1’2026 data.

GMV ROI = maximize ΔGMV/ΔCost, keeping ΔGMV positive as a constraint.

Gross Profit ROI = maximize ΔGross Profit/ΔCost, keeping ΔGMV positive as a constraint.

Optimal DT (Gross Profit + CLTV) = maximize (ΔGross Profit + ΔTotal CLTV), a dollar sum that folds in the reorder-retention channel's lifetime value using Gross Profit.

Optimal DT (GMV+CLTV) ROI = maximize (ΔGMV + ΔTotal CLTV) / ΔTotal Cost — the same GMV+CLTV dollar sum, using GMV instead of Gross Profit for the CLTV channel, expressed as a ROI ratio against MONTHLY cost (both numerator and denominator are monthly, matching GMV ROI/Gross Profit ROI); keeps the same ΔGMV positive constraint.

Overall Gross Profit and CLTV Impacts

P&L from the base DT scenario (base→target DT) and OL scenario (base→target OL) driving every section on this page.
Total GMV = GMV from CVR and Fail Recovery (Reduced DT) + GMV from Fail Recovery (Order Late).
Total Cost = CX-impacted cost + Rider Cost.
Total Revenue = Commission Revenue + Delivery Fee + Service Fee (each this country's own actual % of GMV — see Appendix).
Gross Profit = Revenue − Total Cost.
Four optimization functions:
GMV ROI = ΔGMV/ΔTotal Cost.
Gross Profit ROI = ΔGross Profit/ΔTotal Cost.
Optimal DT (Gross Profit + CLTV) = Gross Profit + Total CLTV.
Optimal DT (GMV+CLTV) ROI = (GMV + Total CLTV) / Total Cost, using GMV in place of Gross Profit for the CLTV channel.
